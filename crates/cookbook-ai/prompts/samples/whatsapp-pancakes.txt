hey here's the pancake recipe you asked for makes about 8. 200g self raising flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1 tbsp sugar, 2 eggs, 300ml milk, knob of butter for the pan. whisk the flour baking powder and sugar, make a well, crack in the eggs and pour in the milk, whisk till smooth. heat a pan with a bit of butter and cook a ladle at a time, about 1-2 mins each side till golden. we have them with lemon and sugar!
