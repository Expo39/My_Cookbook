Lemon tart

Serves 8

Pastry: 200g plain flour, 100g cold butter, 50g icing sugar, 1 egg yolk
Filling: 4 eggs, 150g caster sugar, 150ml double cream, zest and juice of 2 lemons

Rub the butter into the flour and sugar, add the yolk and bring together. Chill for 30 minutes.
Roll out, line a 23cm tin and bake blind at 190C for 15 minutes.
Whisk the eggs, sugar, cream, lemon zest and juice. Pour into the case and bake at 150C for 30-35 minutes until just set.
