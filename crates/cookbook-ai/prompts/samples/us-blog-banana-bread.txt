The Most Amazing Moist Banana Bread You'll Ever Make!

Hi friends! Fall is here and nothing says cozy like a warm loaf fresh out of the oven. My mom used to make this every Sunday... (keep scrolling for the printable recipe card!)

PIN IT FOR LATER!

Yield: 1 loaf (10 slices)
Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 60 minutes

INGREDIENTS
3 ripe bananas, mashed
1/3 cup melted butter
1/2 cup sugar
1 egg, beaten
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 tsp baking soda
Pinch of salt
1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 4x8 inch loaf pan.
2. In a mixing bowl, mash the bananas with a fork until smooth. Stir in the melted butter.
3. Mix in the baking soda and salt. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.
4. Mix in the flour, then fold in the walnuts if using.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan. Bake for 55-65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
6. Let it cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack.
