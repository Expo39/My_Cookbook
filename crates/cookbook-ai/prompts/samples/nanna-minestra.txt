Nanna's minestra (from her notebook)

kunserva - 2 tablespoons
1 onion
2 potatoes
half a small pumpkin
2 carrots
1 kohlrabi (gidra) if you find it
a handful of beans soaked overnight
1 cup small pasta (ditalini)
salt, pepper
grated cheese for the top

fry onion in olive oil till soft. put in the kunserva and stir. cut all the vegetables small and add with the beans. cover with water, about 2 litres. simmer one and a half hours till everything is soft. add the pasta last 10 min. salt and pepper. serve with cheese on top. better the next day.
