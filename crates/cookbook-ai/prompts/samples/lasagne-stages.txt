Lasagne al forno

Serves 6-8. Difficulty: medium.

For the ragù:
2 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, 1 carrot and 1 celery stick, finely chopped
500g beef mince
250g pork mince
150ml red wine
2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
2 bay leaves

For the béchamel:
60g butter
60g plain flour
800ml milk
a pinch of nutmeg

To assemble:
12 sheets dried lasagne
100g Parmesan, grated

The day before:
Heat the oil and soften the onion, carrot and celery for 10 minutes. Brown the mince, then pour in the wine and let it bubble away. Add the tomatoes and bay leaves and simmer gently for 2-3 hours, stirring now and then. Cool and keep in the fridge overnight.

On the day:
Melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Whisk in the milk a little at a time and simmer until thick, about 5 minutes. Season with the nutmeg.
Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Layer ragù, lasagne sheets and béchamel in a dish, three times, finishing with béchamel. Scatter over the Parmesan.
Bake for 40-45 minutes until golden and bubbling, then rest for 15 minutes before cutting.
