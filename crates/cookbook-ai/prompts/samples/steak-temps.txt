Pan-seared ribeye

1 ribeye steak, about 300g and 3cm thick
1 tbsp sunflower oil
30g butter
2 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
2 sprigs thyme
Sea salt and black pepper

Take the steak out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking and season it well with salt and pepper.
Heat a heavy frying pan until very hot, add the oil and lay in the steak. Cook for 3 minutes without moving it, then turn it over.
Add the butter, garlic and thyme, and baste the steak with the foaming butter for 2-3 minutes, until the centre reaches 52°C for medium-rare.
Rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
