The BEST Baked Ricotta Pasta - easy weeknight dinner!!

I've been making this for years and my kids absolutely love it. Scroll down for the recipe card, and don't forget to rate it!

Serves 4. Prep: 15 mins. Cook: 35 mins.

Ingredients
For the pasta:
- 400g rigatoni
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2-3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- Salt and pepper to taste
For the topping:
- 250g ricotta
- 80-100g grated Parmesan
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Fresh basil, to serve (optional)

Method
1. Heat the oven to 180C fan.
2. Cook the rigatoni in a large pan of salted boiling water for 10 minutes, then drain.
3. Meanwhile, warm 1 tbsp olive oil in a frying pan and fry the garlic for 1 minute. Add the tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 10-12 minutes.
4. Stir the pasta into the sauce and tip it into a baking dish.
5. Mix the ricotta with half of the Parmesan and the lemon zest. Spoon it over the pasta and scatter the rest of the Parmesan on top.
6. Bake for 15 minutes, until golden. Scatter with basil to serve.

Tip: you can make the tomato sauce a day ahead and keep it in the fridge.

Nutrition: 610 kcal per serving.
